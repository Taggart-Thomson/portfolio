# About Taggart Thomson

## Who he is

Taggart Thomson is an operator who can also build. He has spent eleven years running operations and businesses, including years in digital media, and he now builds AI and automation tools that teams actually use.

He is not a specialist software engineer, and he says so plainly. His strength is finding the workflow worth fixing, building the first version with AI tools, working with engineers on the production version, and measuring the result.

## Where he is based

Taggart is a Canadian citizen, born and raised in Calgary. He is currently working remotely from Brazil and is relocating to Canada.

He is open to remote roles in Canada and the Americas, and to hybrid roles in Canada. He does not need sponsorship to work in Canada.

## Languages

English is his native language. His Portuguese is intermediate and improving.

## What he is looking for

Taggart is looking for roles where operations experience and hands-on AI building meet:

- forward-deployed and AI deployment roles,
- AI enablement and AI operations roles,
- AI product roles,
- AI transformation consulting.

His next milestone is an AI Systems Engineer role. His long-term goal is a Chief AI Officer track.

# About Taggart Thomson

## Who he is

Taggart Thomson is an operator who can also build. He has spent eleven years running operations and businesses, including years in digital media, and he now builds AI and automation tools that teams actually use.

He starts where the work actually happens: he finds where time or money is being lost, builds the first working version himself with AI tools, puts it in front of the people who will use it, measures the result and fixes what breaks. He is most useful between the business and the engineering team. He has owned budgets, run a program of about 500 students a term and built a company, so he knows what a tool has to deliver, and then he builds it. When it needs to scale, he works with engineers to take it into production, as he did with the Virtual Program Assistant and the Term Scheduler. He learns project first: every tool on his site started as a real problem he needed solved.

## Where he is based

Taggart is a Canadian citizen, born and raised in Calgary. He is currently working remotely from Brazil and is relocating to Canada.

He is open to remote roles in Canada and the Americas, and to hybrid roles in Canada. He does not need sponsorship to work in Canada.

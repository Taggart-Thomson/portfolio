# Current role: Online Program Director

## The role

Since October 2023, Taggart has been Online Program Director at Think Tank Training Centre, a global online VFX and 3D art school based in Canada. He works remotely.

## Scope

He runs the school's online program. Each term that means about 500 students (600 at peak), 22 to 36 supervisors, and 72 mentors, spread across every time zone.

He works daily with academics and compliance, accounting, marketing and engineering, and he reports to the founder.

## Budget ownership

Taggart owns a contractor budget of about CAD 490,000 per term and is relied on for cost-saving measures.

He built the cost model leadership used to approve a new supervisor group structure, and then implemented the new structure himself. It saves about CAD 119,677 per term, which is about CAD 360,000 a year. He owned the whole change: modeling the options, showing leadership the trade-offs and numbers to decide, and rolling it out.

## Running the term build

Taggart runs each term build end to end. For the October 2026 term, he launched 24 supervisor groups on the promised date, with all 22 supervisors confirmed within 48 hours. The Term Scheduler placed just over 220 students into those 24 groups.

## What he has built in this role

In this role he has built:

- a virtual program assistant for students and graduates, which started as a career assistant (see the Virtual Program Assistant project),
- a cost-aware term scheduler for placing students into supervised groups (see Term Scheduler),
- a career hub and placement tracker (see Career Hub),
- a mentor assessment automation in Google Apps Script (see Mentor Assessment Automation),
- 9 HubSpot messaging workflows that he rebuilds and launches each term (see Workflow Automation).

# Career history

## Co-Founder, Try Creative Media Group Ltd., Shanghai (February 2017 to October 2023)

Taggart co-founded Try Creative Media Group, a digital media and video production company in Shanghai. He and his co-founder started with 2 founders and no clients, and grew it to 9 people and more than CNY 4 million a year in revenue.

The company's clients included BMW, Cartier, McLaren, Porsche, Jaguar Land Rover, Kate Spade, Heineken, Rémy Martin, W Hotels, Yves Saint Laurent, L'Oréal, The EDITION hotels, Dom Pérignon, Diageo, Michelin, Marriott Bonvoy and Roland-Garros, among others.

## What Try Creative produced

The company produced campaign content: TV commercials, social ads, launch screens, event recaps, case studies and 3D work. Projects ranged from small shoots to flagship events, including the W Hotel opening and Porsche's 20-year anniversary in China.

## What Taggart owned as co-founder

Taggart owned business development, pricing, budgets, vendors, invoicing and delivery. He presented strategy directly to senior client stakeholders.

During COVID, he restructured the business to keep clients supplied.

## Senior Associate, Head of Digital Content, Red Gate International, Shanghai (October 2015 to February 2017)

At Red Gate International, Taggart led client coordination on international PR events and brand campaigns, and produced their digital content. He also reviewed contracts, reports and proposals for accuracy and compliance.

## Years in China

In total, Taggart lived and worked in China for about 8 years.

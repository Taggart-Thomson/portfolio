# How Taggart works

## Start with how the work actually gets done

Taggart starts by looking at how the work really happens, then finds where time or money is being lost. He builds the first version, puts it in front of real users, measures it, and fixes what breaks.

## Comfortable being wrong early

He is comfortable being wrong early. The AI Career Assistant's first approach, a rules-based extractor, failed on real transcripts. Fixing it taught him more than the demo did, and the switch to an LLM-based extractor is the version the school's engineering team later adopted as the model for production.

## Learning project first

Taggart came to coding from zero. Opening the terminal for the first time was the starting point. He still learns this way: pick a real problem, build the project, and learn what the project needs.

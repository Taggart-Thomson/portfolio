# Driver's license exam trainer

## The problem

Taggart needed to pass Brazil's written driver's license exam (DETRAN-MG) as a native English speaker taking the test in Portuguese. Many questions turn on a single word, so knowing the rule in English isn't enough.

## What he built

He built a learning app that teaches the rules in English alongside the Portuguese "signal words" that decide each question, such as *exceto* (except) and *proibido* (prohibited).

## How it works

The app has six sections, each with lessons, worked examples and practice quizzes. A section is marked complete at 70 percent or higher, and a progress ring tracks the whole course.

The mock exam mirrors the real test: 30 timed questions in 30 minutes, drawn from each section in the same proportions as the official paper and reshuffled on every attempt. Passing takes 21 correct answers, and the app reviews every missed question afterward.

It is an installable PWA and works offline.

## Status

The app is live on GitHub Pages. Its question bank is being rewritten with original questions based on Brazil's public traffic code (CTB) before it is linked from this site.

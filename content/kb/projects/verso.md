# Verso

## What it is

Verso is an internal web app Taggart built for a public art organization. It combines a searchable artwork collection with a contractor invoicing workflow.

## The collection

The collection holds 257 works, imported from a confidential spreadsheet.

## The invoicing workflow

The workflow spans 3 legal entities and 4 roles. A contractor submits an invoice, an entity reviewer approves it or returns it, accounting records the payment, and admins manage accounts.

## How it is built

Django and SQL, with email-based accounts and PDF and Excel exports. It has 24 passing tests.

## What changed after user review

Taggart reviewed the prototype with its users and redesigned the workflow based on what he learned.

## Status

Verso is a working prototype and is not yet deployed. Because the client's data is confidential, it is shown only as a diagram.

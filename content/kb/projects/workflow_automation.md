# HubSpot workflow automation

## What Taggart runs

Taggart owns the school's student messaging in HubSpot (Marketing Hub Professional). Every term he builds and launches 9 automated workflows that carry students through onboarding, orientations, reminders and webinars, timed to that term's dates.

## How he manages them

He rebuilds every workflow each term: new send dates, links and enrollment lists, then he tests and launches them. Once live, enrollment and sends run automatically. He builds the emails in HTML and sets up the triggered sends. A Zoom webinar integration enrolls every member automatically, so nobody has to be added by hand. He also manages the static lists and the support ticket queue, which he works through with the team twice a week.

## What's next

He has scoped a phase that automates the term rebuild itself, with timing relative to each student's enrollment and lists created through the API.

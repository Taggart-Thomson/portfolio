## The problem

I needed to pass Brazil's written driver's license exam (DETRAN-MG) as a native English speaker taking the test in Portuguese. Many questions turn on a single word, so knowing the rule in English isn't enough.

## What I built

A learning app that teaches the rules in English alongside the Portuguese "signal words" that decide each question, such as *exceto* (except) and *proibido* (prohibited).

## How it works

Six sections, each with lessons, worked examples and practice quizzes. A section is complete at 70 percent or higher, and a progress ring tracks the whole course.

The mock exam mirrors the real test: 30 timed questions in 30 minutes, drawn from each section in the same proportions as the official paper and reshuffled on every attempt. Passing takes 21 correct answers, and the app reviews every missed question afterward.

It installs like an app and works offline.
